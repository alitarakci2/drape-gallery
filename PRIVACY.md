# Drape privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Drape is a wallpaper app for Windows published by Indio Gaming. This page says what it does with your data. The short version: it collects none.

## What Drape does not do

- It has no accounts and asks for no personal information.
- It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- It sends nothing about you, your files or how you use the app to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your computer

Your settings, your favorites, the images you hid and a short activity log are kept in files on your own computer. The wallpapers are files in folders you choose. Removing the app, and those folders if you wish, removes all of it.

## When Drape uses the internet

Drape goes online for two things, both part of its Gallery page:

- It reads the list of gallery images from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`).
- When you ask for a set, it downloads those images from Wikimedia Commons (`upload.wikimedia.org`).

Like any download, these requests show your IP address and the app's name and version to those services. What they do with that is described in their own privacy policies: [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and the [Wikimedia Foundation](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). If you never open the Gallery page, Drape does not go online.

## The Microsoft Store

If you install Drape from the Microsoft Store, the Store handles the download, updates and any purchase under [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Drape itself does not see your Microsoft account or payment details.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new text and date appear on this page. Questions can be asked by opening an issue in this repository.
