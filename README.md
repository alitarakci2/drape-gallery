# Drape gallery

The list of wallpapers the Drape app offers on its Gallery page.

`catalog.json` holds one entry per image: its category and set, a title, a short description, who made it, its licence, where it comes from and where to download it. The images themselves are not stored here; they are freely licensed files on Wikimedia Commons, 4K or larger, and the app downloads them from there.

Each image keeps its own licence and credit, named in its entry. If an entry is wrong or you want an image of yours taken out, open an issue.
